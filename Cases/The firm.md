## The firm

Costa & Vale is a 14-person accountancy in Porto. Two partners (Inês Costa, tax; Rui Vale, accounts), eight accountants, two juniors, one office manager (Teresa), one part-time IT contractor.

Clients: ~180 SMEs and self-employed people. Work is seasonal: VAT every month, CIT and personal tax in the spring peak.

They already pay for Sage (books) and a shared Google Drive. Several staff use personal ChatGPT to rewrite emails. Inês is uneasy about client data in consumer tools. A software vendor offered them an “AI accountant agent that files and emails clients on its own.” Rui is tempted because they cannot hire another senior before April.

**You are a BA student on a two-week project.** The partners asked for a bounded pilot, not a transformation plan.

## The messy reality (read twice)

**Workflow under pressure: client questions in inbox and WhatsApp**  
Teresa and the assigned accountant answer the same things all day: “When is VAT due?”, “Can I deduct the new laptop?”, “What documents do you still need?”, “Why is my draft bill so high?”

- Volume: ~70 questions per week in peak, ~25 off-peak.  
- About 40% are repeats of last year or of the firm’s own emails.  
- About 30% need a look at that client’s folder.  
- About 20% are judgment (grey deductions, “is this allowed?”).  
- About 10% are complaints or fee disputes.

Today the path is: message arrives → Teresa forwards → accountant searches Drive / Sage / memory → types a reply → sometimes Inês reviews if the client is large. Average first reply: **11 hours**. Repeat questions: **one in four** clients ask again because the first answer was vague.

**Other pains the partners mentioned** (do **not** solve all of them in this canvas):
- Juniors spend Fridays assembling document-request lists.  
- VAT return preparation is slow in March.  
- Rui wants dashboards.  
- The vendor demo filed a dummy VAT return in one click.

## Constraints the partners already set

1. No system may file a return or send a bill.  
2. Client data must not go into a personal consumer account.  
3. A partner still signs anything that is tax advice, not a deadline reminder.  
4. Budget for a first test: **€150/month** and **two Fridays**.  
5. They will not replace Teresa.

## Quality bar (how you will be marked)

- **Specific process**, not “digitise the firm.”  
- **Commit boundary** is named (a person, not “the AI”).  
- **Data** is official or you said you will write it first.  
- **Kill criterion** is observable in weeks.  
- You resisted the vendor agent unless the control boxes are stronger than the demo.

**Automatic low mark:** unsupervised client email, filing a return, or “roll out Copilot to everyone.”
